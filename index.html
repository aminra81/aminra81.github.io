A little background

I’m a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Yale University. My research interests span proof systems, post-quantum cryptography, cryptography for AI, and system security.

Before Yale, I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science from Sharif University of Technology. Along the way, I’ve worked with researchers at EPFL, the University of California, Davis, and the University of Maryland.
